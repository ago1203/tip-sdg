SDG 1: T.I.P. commits to breaking the cycle of poverty by expanding scholarship and financial assistance programs, strengthening industry linkages for employability, and supporting community livelihood and technology-transfer projects.
SDG 2: T.I.P. pledges to support food security initiatives through agricultural engineering innovations, community gardens, food technology research, and partnerships that promote sustainable food production and responsible consumption.
SDG 3: T.I.P. ensures a safe, healthy, and inclusive environment for students and employees by maintaining high standards of laboratory and workplace safety, providing mental health support, and promoting health through campus activities.
SDG 4: T.I.P. upholds its mission to transform students into globally competitive professionals through outcomes-based education, continuous faculty development, research integration, and lifelong learning opportunities.
SDG 5: T.I.P. promotes an equitable learning and working environment where all genders have equal access to education, leadership, and advancement opportunities, ensuring policies that eliminate discrimination and harassment.
SDG 6: T.I.P. commits to ensuring access to safe and potable water across all campuses, maintaining proper sanitation facilities, and promoting water conservation through sustainable plumbing, wastewater management, and educational initiatives for students.
SDG 7: T.I.P. pledges to improve energy efficiency in its laboratories, buildings, and offices, promote renewable energy technologies in its curriculum and research, and adopt sustainable practices that reduce the institutional carbon footprint.
SDG 8: T.I.P. supports fair and safe employment practices for faculty and staff, promotes entrepreneurship and innovation among students, and fosters partnerships with industry that lead to meaningful work and inclusive economic opportunities.
SDG 9: T.I.P. commits to strengthening its Technopreneurship and Collaborative Research (TechnoCoRe) programs, developing innovative and sustainable laboratories, and driving innovation that supports resilient infrastructure and responsible industrial growth.
SDG 10: T.I.P. ensures that education and institutional services are accessible to all regardless of background, and promotes inclusivity through scholarships, accessible facilities, and equal opportunities in learning and employment.
SDG 11: T.I.P. pledges to make its campuses models of sustainability through green building initiatives, safe learning environments, disaster resilience planning, and community engagement in sustainable urban development.
SDG 12: T.I.P. commits to responsible classroom and office operations by implementing proper waste management, sustainable procurement, and equipment lifecycle management while promoting resource efficiency and environmental consciousness.
SDG 13: T.I.P. pledges to reduce its environmental impact through energy conservation, greening initiatives, and climate education, while integrating disaster risk reduction and climate resilience into its institutional operations and curricula.
SDG 14: T.I.P. supports initiatives for water conservation, pollution prevention, and research that protect marine and freshwater ecosystems, especially in communities affected by environmental degradation and industrial waste.
SDG 15: T.I.P. commits to promoting biodiversity conservation and sustainable landscaping within its campuses through tree planting, green spaces, and environmental awareness programs that foster ecological responsibility.
SDG 16: T.I.P. upholds transparency, accountability, and ethical governance in all its institutional processes, fostering a culture of peace, integrity, and responsible citizenship among students and employees.
SDG 17: T.I.P. pledges to strengthen collaborations with industry, academe, government, and international partners to advance research, education, and outreach initiatives that accelerate the achievement of the SDGs.
